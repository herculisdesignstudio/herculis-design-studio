Our story

Design that respects your everyday

Herculis Design Studio began with a frustration: most "productivity" downloads looked like an afterthought. We set out to make planners, journals, and templates that are as well-designed as anything you'd frame — and durable enough to actually use every week.

What we believe

Good design isn't reserved for big-budget products. A grocery list or a lesson planner deserves the same intention as a magazine spread.

How we work

Every product is sketched, tested by real parents, teachers, and business owners, then refined based on how people actually use it — not just how it looks in a preview.

Where we're headed

We're building out the catalog by listening to what our community keeps asking for — new categories launch based on real requests, not guesses.
